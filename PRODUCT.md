# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences, all in the middle of a real construction or land project, looking for someone reliable to do the digging:

- **Homeowners** building or renovating a house, needing foundations dug or utilities connected. Often first-time builders, anxious about cost and being misled, want clarity and someone who shows up.
- **Private investors** developing plots or small buildings, comparing contractors, value proof of past work and seriousness over marketing polish.
- **Small construction contractors** who subcontract earthworks, need a dependable crew with its own equipment, judge competence by the quality of finished work in photos.

Context of use: usually on a phone, often mid-project or while planning, frequently in a hurry to get a quote. The decisive moment is "can I trust these people to do it right." Conversion is a phone call or WhatsApp message, not a long form.

## Product Purpose

A one-page marketing site for **SC REG Excavații Profesionale SRL** that turns real worksite photos into trust, and trust into a phone call or WhatsApp message.

The site exists to prove the company does serious, correct excavation work, using only its own real project photos as evidence, then make contacting them effortless. Success = the visitor believes these are competent, serious people and taps call or WhatsApp.

Tagline: *Lucrări de săpătură executate corect, cu seriozitate și claritate.*

Services, each backed by a real photo set from the company's own worksites:
- Branșamente apă (water connections)
- Branșamente canalizare (sewer connections)
- Branșamente gaz (gas connections)
- Fundații case (house foundations)
- Îndepărtare moluz (debris / rubble removal)
- Nivelare teren (land leveling)
- Săpături electrice (electrical trenching)

Service area: nationwide (România), wording adapted to project scope and location.

## Brand Personality

Serious, exact, no-hype. Three words: **dependable, precise, grounded.**

Voice: plain Romanian, direct, technically credible without jargon-dumping. Speaks like an experienced operator who respects the client's money and time. Never salesy, never inflated claims, never exclamation-mark energy. Confidence comes from showing finished work, not adjectives.

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel *relief* ("finally, someone who clearly knows what they're doing and won't mess me around") and *certainty* enough to call.

## Anti-references

- Generic local-tradesman template sites: repeated identical service cards with a stock icon, heading, and three lines of filler text. This is the explicit thing to avoid.
- Stock-photo construction sites (smiling models in clean hard hats, generic excavator clip art). Only real project photos are allowed.
- Hype-driven marketing tone: "#1 best", "leaders in", exclamation marks, fake urgency badges.
- Heavy corporate-SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric counters. None of that fits a real earthworks crew.
- Cluttered, ad-heavy directory-listing aesthetics.

## Design Principles

1. **Photos are the argument.** Real worksite images are the primary trust-building content, not decoration. Every service section is built around its own image set as proof of work. Layout serves the photos.
2. **No repeated card grid.** Each service category is its own proof-driven section with distinct layout rhythm and image composition. Monotony reads as a template; a template reads as untrustworthy.
3. **Conversion is one tap away, always.** Phone-first and WhatsApp-first. The call/WhatsApp action is reachable from anywhere on the page without hunting.
4. **Show, don't claim.** Earn belief through finished work and exact language, never through superlatives or marketing inflation.
5. **Respect the visitor's hurry.** Fast to load, fast to scan, fast to act. They are mid-project on a phone, not browsing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: sufficient contrast on all text and CTAs, visible focus states, keyboard-operable.
- Mobile-first: the majority of visitors arrive on a phone; touch targets and click-to-call must be effortless.
- Every worksite photo gets meaningful Romanian alt text describing the work shown, since the images carry the core message.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any gallery or reveal animation.
